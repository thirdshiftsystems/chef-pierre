# Eggs-in-Wells Skillet Technique

## When to Use

Cooking any dish that cracks eggs into wells over a cooked filling — a spiced
meat skillet (kıymalı yumurta), tomato-pepper base (shakshuka / eggs in
purgatory), or a bean/salsa bed (huevos rancheros) — especially when the cook
reports the whites aren't setting. The goal on every one: **whites fully set,
yolks still soft**. Two rules get there; both were learned live on a kıymalı
yumurta cook where the whites would not set.

## The two rules

1. **Scrape each well all the way down to the bare pan.** The egg must sit
   directly on hot metal, not on the filling. An egg resting on the filling
   cooks only by ambient heat + trapped steam, so its white stays translucent
   and gooey for minutes while the yolk overcooks in the wait. On the recipe
   card write **"scrape to the bare metal"**, never "make a well with a spoon"
   (the spoon well leaves the egg floating on the bed).

2. **Start the eggs at MEDIUM, then turn down.** The white needs an initial
   blast of pan heat to set fast. Beginning on medium-low is the classic
   failure — after 6+ minutes the white is still jelly and the yolk is on its
   way to cooked because of the wait. Correct sequence: crack eggs in, keep
   heat at MEDIUM, cover, ~2–3 min until the white sets around the edges; then
   drop to medium-low and finish covered ~2–3 min so the trapped steam sets the
   top of the white without hardening the yolk.

## Rescue order (when the whites won't set)

In order, no starting over:
1. **Raise the heat to medium** (the pan was almost certainly too cold).
2. **Keep the lid on, and don't peek once** — every lift of the lid bleeds the
   steam that finishes the top of the white.
3. Give it **2–4 more minutes covered**.
4. Last resort: flip each egg once for ~20–30 seconds to set the white side.

If the eggs collapse into a loose scramble with the filling, that is the
welcome rustic version — the flavor is unaffected, so present it as a feature,
not a failure.

## Pitfalls

- **Starting too cold is the #1 cause of unset whites** — the fix is raising
  the heat, not waiting longer. Waiting overcooks the yolk while the white
  still won't set.
- **Crowding is not the problem**; a pan crowded with 4 eggs against a layer
  of filling sets fine once the heat is right. Don't blame the pan size — fix
  the heat and the well depth.
- **The low-mess, one-pan format makes this family a strong weeknight pick**
  (a 10/10 in this household): one skillet, no sheet pan, no extra vessels.
