# Toad-in-a-Hole

## Classic Method
- Butter or mayo one side of a bread slice; place butter-side down in a hot skillet.
- Use a drinking glass to cut a round hole in the center; set the round aside.
- Crack an egg into the hole; cook until bottom sets, then flip once.
- Optional: add cheese after flipping, cover briefly to melt.
- Toast the cut-out round alongside; serve it alongside or dipped.

## Variations
1. **Bacon & Cheddar** — add cooked bacon pieces with the cheese after flipping.
2. **Pesto & Mozzarella** — spread a thin layer of pesto before adding egg; use fresh mozzarella.
3. **Hot Sausage & Pepper Jack** — crumble cooked sausage with red pepper flakes before egg; top with pepper jack.
4. **Everything Bagel** — brush mayo side with everything bagel seasoning before skillet; top with cream cheese after flipping.
5. **Tomato & Feta** — add diced cherry tomatoes with egg; finish with feta and a drizzle of olive oil.
6. **Maple & Cinnamon** — use butter, not mayo; sprinkle cinnamon sugar before flipping; serve as a sweet breakfast.

## Equipment Notes
- Skillet: cast iron preferred; medium heat.
- No microwave: reheat leftovers in toaster oven or stovetop.
