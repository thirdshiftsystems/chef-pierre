# Çılbır (Turkish Poached Eggs with Garlic Yogurt & Chili Butter)

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 20 min · **Difficulty:** easy · **Format:** stovetop skillet
**Why it's here:** the sleeper hit of the whole vault — a 10/10 from someone who does not hand out 10s. Poached eggs over cold garlicky yogurt, finished with sizzling paprika butter. Cold + hot, creamy + fiery.

## Ingredients

- 4 large eggs (as fresh as possible — they poach cleanly)
- 1 cup plain whole-milk Greek yogurt (Fage 2% or 5% both work)
- 1 small clove garlic, pressed or finely grated (a garlic press is the tool for this)
- Pinch of salt
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter
- 1 tsp sweet paprika (or half sweet + half hot, or Aleppo/Gochugaru)
- Pinch of red pepper flakes
- Toasted crusty bread (country loaf, sourdough, or pita), for serving

## Method

1. **Yogurt base:** stir the yogurt, garlic, and salt together in a bowl. Let it sit at room temperature while you cook — cold yogurt with hot eggs is the point, so don't chill it further.
2. **Poach:** bring a wide pan of water to a bare simmer (small bubbles, not a boil). Crack each egg into a small cup, swirl the water, and slide the eggs in. Poach 3 minutes for runny yolks. Lift with a slotted spoon and drain briefly.
   - *Egg-cooker shortcut:* a Dash-style egg cooker can't poach in the traditional sense, but poach cups work — 3–4 min.
3. **Chili butter:** melt the butter in a small skillet over medium heat until it foams, then add the paprika and pepper flakes. Swirl 30 seconds — fragrant and brick-red, do NOT let the paprika brown or it turns bitter.
4. **Plate:** spread the garlicky yogurt on two plates, nest the drained eggs on top, and spoon the sizzling chili butter over everything.
5. Serve immediately with toasted bread for dragging through the mess.

## Notes

- The contrast is the dish: cold yogurt, hot eggs, screaming butter. Don't let any component wait around.
- Bread quality matters more than it should — a toasted country loaf beats sandwich bread every time.
- Dill (fresh, chopped) on top is a legitimate variation.
